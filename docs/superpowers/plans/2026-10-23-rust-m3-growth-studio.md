## Review Focus

1. A strategy replacement/archive or group removal races a queued publish/budget change: use the new frame or refuse before I/O, while retained hypotheses keep learning → T3 `strategy_change_racing_dispatch_uses_current_frame`, T8 `group_removed_before_budget_write_refuses`.
2. Provider accepted a post but the response was lost, including after restart: persist uncertain outcome and never blindly publish again → T6 `accepted_publish_response_lost_never_blindly_retries`.
3. Two workers spend the same remaining cap; absolute daily-budget updates or replayed metrics must not double-count spend → T8 `concurrent_reservations_never_exceed_cap`, `absolute_budget_is_not_spend`, `spend_reconciliation_is_idempotent`.
4. Two sets share one campaign, and one measurement is missing: neither campaign totals nor missing counters may become arm reward → T7 `shared_campaign_totals_never_credit_two_arms`, `missing_metrics_do_not_reward_zero`.
5. Brake is pressed while both workers are poised to dispatch, then a process restarts: no new publish/budget dispatch beyond the next iteration boundary, and another tenant remains unaffected → T3 `brake_stops_both_lanes_at_iteration_boundary`, `brake_survives_restart_and_is_tenant_scoped`.
