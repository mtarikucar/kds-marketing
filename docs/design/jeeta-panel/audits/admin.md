# Administration and provider review

Baseline: `d5b8d296`. Tenant tools use compact, legible data rows, with a distinct action area and contextual detail under each capability. Provider facts and policy controls are separated by rules inside their parent capability instead of another stack of bordered boxes. Long identifiers and operational paths wrap on small screens. Platform pages retain a distinct operator frame. No provider connection, payment, or account action was sent to an external service during this review.

## Browser evidence

`rust/panel/design-review/admin.cjs` signs in to a disposable local workspace and only opens dialogs. It captured `/team`, `/roles`, `/api-keys`, `/agents`, `/budget`, `/ledger`, `/billing`, `/capabilities`, and `/connect` at 1440 and 390 pixels. The invite, create role, create budget, and declare capability dialogs were opened at both widths. The local API-key page was accessed through in-app navigation because the original Vite proxy intercepted direct `/api-keys` requests; the synthetic run covers its dialogs. None of these observed pages or dialogs had document overflow. Screenshots are generated under `/tmp/jeeta-admin-review` by default; account path and server can be overridden with `ADMIN_ACCOUNT` and `ADMIN_BASE`.

`rust/panel/design-review/admin-fixtures.cjs` intercepts all API requests and supplies synthetic responses for supported writes; unsupported writes are rejected locally. Synthetic fixture data renders capability status, connector health, PBX policy, phone details, voice bridge facts, groups, webhook addresses, and platform workspace/payment/health views. It opens capability edit, policy, unreachable, budget-link and retire dialogs, the workspace suspension dialog, and each of 13 provider choices at both widths. These captured views had no document overflow. Screenshots go to `/tmp/jeeta-admin-fixtures`. The same isolated harness captured billing waiting, reported, activation, paid and revoked states, and OAuth expired, missing, denied, unknown-provider and asset-selection states at both widths, plus API-key revoke and one-time-secret dialogs. These captures had no document overflow. Fixture results are not evidence of a live provider or operator connection.

## Workflow verification and limits

The focused single-worker Vitest run covers tenant administration, billing, capability/provider workflows, OAuth callback, phone bridge, and platform pages. It exercises create/edit/remove confirmations, role and permission gating, provider credential and OAuth error paths, PBX policy, phone pairing and command details, webhook visibility, and payment evidence/activation handling through mocked responses. These are component and behavior tests, not visual browser coverage of every result. The scripts do not submit provider credentials, activate billing, create or revoke accounts, or reach external services. The dedicated fixture passes below cover the distinct loading, error, restricted-role, one-time-result and confirmation views. They do not enumerate every possible combination of permissions or backend errors; the recorded scenarios identify the branches actually rendered.

## Recorded results

The consolidated [machine-readable result file](../../../../rust/panel/design-review/admin-results.json) has 201 route/case/width records: 24 real-workspace page/dialog records, 61 initial synthetic provider/operator records, 10 callback records, 4 key records, 2 one-time invite records, 56 loading/error/restricted-role records, 28 create/edit/confirmation records, 12 additional error records, and 4 synthetic operator payment delivery/revocation records. These include repeated cases from separate runs; they are not 201 unique screens. All recorded widths stayed within the viewport. The captured confirmation set includes member removal and invitation cancellation, role create/edit/delete, key create/revoke, budget create/edit/spend/retire, and operator suspend/revoke. The one-time key and invitation screens were inspected at phone width; no secret was real. The initial real `/api-keys` direct navigation was excluded from the results because the Vite proxy intercepted that URL. Synthetic key checks used in-app navigation. The final Vite proxy fix matches only the `/api` path segment, and the root real-API sweep subsequently verified direct `/api-keys` navigation at both widths.

The fixture harness accepts `ADMIN_RESULTS` to write flat results and page errors for future runs, and exits nonzero on an observed page error or document overflow. Some earlier captures predate page-error collection; their absence of page errors is not asserted. External provider authentication and real payment or operator writes were never performed. Individual screenshot inspection included provider choices, capability phone/PBX/voice bridge/group/webhook surfaces and policy dialog, operator health and payment, billing reported/activation, OAuth assets/expired, key/invite one-time screens, and a contact sheet of the confirmation dialogs.
