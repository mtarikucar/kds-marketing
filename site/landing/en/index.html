How it works

Your accounts.
Your agent.

Jeeta has no built-in model generating content on its own. The agent you connect does that work. Jeeta keeps the work record, checks the rules and handles delivery through connected accounts.

Connect your accounts
You connect your business email and social accounts to the workspace. The agent works with the access you grant.
Connect your agent
You set up the connection on the Agents page. Your connected agent prepares reply text, content and advertising plans.
Choose where approval applies
Comment replies and social posts wait when human approval is enabled on the connection. This setting does not cover every kind of sending; it does not mean every email or private message waits for approval.
Review advertising proposals
A new advertising campaign proposed by an agent needs human approval. When you open a campaign yourself with a goal and budget, there is no separate proposal approval.
